Wartime boy 1940-41
1940: 16-year old Jim is worried.  Will his house survive August bombing raids? Did he revise enough for school finals? (And does this matter now that invasion seems imminent?)  
1941: will Selene fall for Jim in his Home Guard boots and bayonet?
  Home Guard, 1941-2
17-year-old Jim has joined his dad in the Home Guard (Local Defence Volunteers). But will they let the alarmingly keen recruit anywhere near the live hand grenades? 
    Army life, 1942- 3
Summer of ‘42: 18-year-old recruit Jim records news of the fighting at Alamein and Stalingrad - along with his own war against route marches, blanco,‘bullshit’ and Broadbent the barrack room bully
200 free cigarettes - and a ‘no smoking’ troop deck; shipwreck threatens; first time in action; to Rome via the ruins of Cassino;

More than 100 illustrations are used in this site: photos, war art, cartoons, posters, maps and documents. Some are from my family collection, others from various sources, notably London’s Imperial War Museum. Where archive pictures are used (as in the action photos above) most are carefully chosen to illustrate diary events.
         Sources are stated where known, but I have not sought formal permission to use any of the pictures.  If any copyright holders object to my use of your pictures I apologise and will remove them at once.
         This page features an allied wartime map of the Gothic Line and one of the Italy star medals awarded by Britain to soldiers who served in that country. It also features a photo of my father (date unknown) and (above) a photo of him with his training squadron at Catterick camp, Yorkshire in 1942.
      To the left is a World War 1 era photo, with Jim’s father Bill at the right of the front row.

A boozy Christmas 1944; Greece and communists; V.E. Day, vino and Verey lights;  to post-war Austria, the wild frontier (die-hard SS prisoners, Cossacks, guns, waggon trains, and horse-rustling); Possy’s exploding latrine; general election; V.J. Day
Christmas 1945: return to Rotherham -Jim’s first home leave in two years.
